# 3 thú khởi đầu (hệ Thường + hệ phụ), prompt bản chốt v4

Thú khởi đầu KHÔNG thuộc vùng nào (không nằm trong 17 vùng x 10 thú), hiện ở màn Chọn Thú Đồng Hành. Mỗi con 1 ảnh xếp ngang 3 bậc (Bậc 1 bên TRÁI).
Hệ: Thường làm hệ chủ, hệ phụ khác nhau (người dùng chốt 21/11/2026: Hỏa, Thủy, Mộc; khóa hệ trong game: hoa, thuy, moc). Vai đề xuất (đổi được ở 56b-loai.js): Hỏa = công, Thủy = nhanh, Mộc = tank.
Thân thú = màu Thường (kem + nâu cát) xen mảng ĐEN + màu hệ phụ, nên 3 con cùng "họ" nhưng nhìn là ra hệ.
Dáng khác hẳn nhau: 01 thú bốn chân mảnh (sư tử đá), 02 cá có cánh lơ lửng trên không, 03 heo rừng thấp nặng có cây mọc trên lưng.
Tên chưa kiểm trùng với tên quái cũ (Viêm Sư, Lưu Diêu, Lâm Trư): cần kiểm trước khi đưa vào game.

Màu nền (mỗi con một màu ngược tông, công cụ tách nền nhận mọi màu phẳng): 01 teal #00C2C2 (thú đỏ / cam / kem), 02 và 03 magenta #FF00FF (thú xanh lam / xanh lá).
Tên file ảnh: khoi-dau-01-viem-su.png, khoi-dau-02-luu-dieu.png, khoi-dau-03-lam-tru.png

## 01 · Viêm Sư (Thường + Hỏa, công)
```
Pokémon-style pixel art evolution line sprite sheet of a DIVINE SPIRIT BEAST
from a Vietnamese-Chinese cultivation (tu tien) fantasy game. ONE original
creature inspired by a stylized Chinese guardian-lion cub (a stone shishi lion
with curled mane tufts) fused with a fire spirit, NOT a realistic lion, NOT a
maned fire-lion Pokémon, NOT a dog, NOT a fox, NOT a cat in 3 evolution
stages side by side in a single row, left to right = Stage 1 on the LEFT,

three sections, all in side view facing left, all on the same ground line,
clear empty space between stages, nothing overlapping.

patches so gold and ember details pop against the dark parts. NO teal, NO cyan,
NO green, NO blue anywhere on the creature.

SURFACE DETAIL (must be clearly visible on Stage 2 and especially Stage 3;
Stage 1 shows a simpler version of the same texture): EVERY mane tuft drawn as
an individual curled lock with a visible center line and a lighter edge, in
overlapping rows; short fur strands on the legs and face; cracked-stone
patterns on the shoulders and back; ridged claws and paw pads.
Creature concept: a four-legged lion on all four legs, walking pose, big mane
of curled tufts around the head, a tail ending in a tuft, claws on the paws,
standing on the ground line.
- Stage 1 (baby): chibi proportions: a very big round head, short stubby limbs,
  small soft rounded body, big round cute eyes, a small fluffy mane with a few
  crimson tufts, a few simple markings mixing black and cream, one tiny
  accessory (a tiny gold bell on a thin red cord around the neck).
- Stage 2 (teen): NOT an enlarged copy of Stage 1. The body proportions CHANGE:
  smaller head relative to the body, longer legs and neck, a leaner stronger
  build, sharper angular shapes, NEW body parts appear: the mane grows into 7
  big curled tufts, a pair of short curved horns on the brow, the tail grows
  longer with an ember-tipped tuft, claws grow longer.
  Also glowing gold rune markings, a gold crest on the forehead and gold bracers
  on the front legs, partial black ornamental armor (a shoulder plate), and a
  little of the region effect (a few ember sparks, one or two small outlined
  flame wisps at the mane tips, thin lava-crack lines).
- Stage 3 (adult): NOT an enlarged copy of Stage 2. A completely mature, heroic,
  menacing body with a NEW silhouette: a huge layered mane of curled flame-tufts
  forming a crown around the head, a spiked brow crest, an armored chest plate
  of black stone, broad shoulder pauldrons, big armored paws with stone claws,
  a long tail ending in 3 flame-tufts, a ridge of tufts running down the spine.
  It has the MOST DETAIL and the MOST VARIETY of all stages: every area of the
  body gets a DIFFERENT kind of detail (head, neck, shoulders, back, chest, limbs
  and tail each look different), mixing textures (fur / scale / plate / stone),
  engraved patterns, at least three different rune scripts and patterns, gem
  inlays of different sizes, chains, tassels, spikes, bands, banners, glowing
  cracks, scars, layered armor of different materials, and asymmetrical
  details; ornate accessories (a black-and-gold crown crest, a gold necklace
  with a crimson gem, anklet rings, a hanging gold bell and red tassels); many
  region effects (ember sparks, small outlined flame wisps, glowing lava-crack
  lines, floating hot coals, small outlined ash-stone chunks) around it. Keep
  the silhouette and face readable: effects stay around the body, never cover
  the face or eyes.
EYES: Stage 1 has big round cute eyes with a bright highlight. Stages 2 and 3
have FIERCE, AGGRESSIVE eyes: narrow angled eyes under a heavy dark brow ridge
slanting down toward the nose, sharp slit-like pupils, a bright gold glowing
iris, a menacing glare (Stage 3 the most ferocious, with a thin gold light
line). Every eye is still clearly readable, at least 3x2 pixels, with a bright
highlight on a dark socket, same gold color on all stages.
All 3 stages share the same species, the same color scheme (black, cream,
crimson, gold) and the same key markings, but the body shape and proportions
evolve. Stage 1 is about half the height of Stage 3. Original design, NOT
resembling any existing Pokémon.

FIRE AND LINE RULE: all detail lines (engraved rune lines, armor edges, fur and
mane edges, lava cracks) must be CLEAN, SHARP and CLEARLY VISIBLE: at least 2
pixels wide, continuous (no broken scribbles), high contrast, pale yellow-white
or gold lines drawn on the black and dark-crimson parts, light cream edge lines
on the dark parts. Fire is drawn as a few solid flat flame shapes with a clear
dark outline, always growing from the mane tips, tail tip or paws (always
TOUCHING the creature), plus a few engraved lava-crack lines. NO soft glowing
blobs, NO big fat cartoon fire icons, NO huge flame walls, NO detached floating
flames. Gold covers at most 10% of the creature (small accents only); the body
stays mostly black, cream and crimson. Dense texture and many fine details are
wanted as long as the main silhouette and the face stay clear.

NO text, NO letters, NO numbers, NO writing anywhere (runes are abstract glyphs
only).

Background: one flat solid vivid teal #00C2C2. The background color must NOT
appear anywhere on the creature, not even as a tint. No labels, no arrows, no
shadow, no gradient, no frame.
```
