# Mẫu prompt 1 con (bản chốt v4, vùng Lôi Vực, con 01 Lôi Ưng)

Người dùng đã duyệt: "prompt quá đỉnh, thú đẹp lắm". Các phiên Claude khác bắt chước cấu trúc và độ chi tiết này khi soạn 10 prompt vùng mới. Phần nào là riêng từng con (SURFACE DETAIL, bộ phận mới Bậc 2 / 3, phụ kiện) phải viết lại theo từng con.

## 01 · Lôi Ưng  (file ảnh: loi-vuc-01-loi-ung.png)
```
Pokémon-style pixel art evolution line sprite sheet of a DIVINE SPIRIT BEAST
from a Vietnamese-Chinese cultivation (tu tien) fantasy game. ONE original
creature inspired by a stylized thunder eagle, wings shaped like lightning bolts, NOT a realistic eagle, NOT a phoenix in 3 evolution stages side by side in a single
row, left to right = Stage 1 on the LEFT, Stage 2 in the middle, Stage 3 on
the RIGHT (largest). Wide landscape image, three sections, all in side view
facing left, all on the same ground line (or floating if it flies), clear
empty space between stages, nothing overlapping.

Art style: highly detailed pixel art, crisp hard pixel edges, NO blur. Every
effect (runes, crystals, clouds, sparks, lightning) is drawn as solid opaque
pixel shapes, NO soft glow, NO halo, NO transparency, NO circle or aura behind
the creature. The SAME thick dark navy-black outline on ALL three stages, same
line weight, clearly visible on Stage 3 too. Rich cel shading with 5-6 shades
per color area. Palette: near-black obsidian #0E0A1F (armor plates, underbelly, horns, claws, hooves, patches of body, about 35-40% of the creature), deep indigo #2A1B5E and violet #5B3FA8 (main body), light violet #9B7BFF (highlights, crystals), bright lightning gold #FFD23F and pale yellow-white #FFF3B0 (lightning bolts, runes, eyes, accessories, sparks). Black and violet are interleaved in patches so gold and crystal details pop against the dark parts. NO green, NO teal, NO cyan anywhere on the creature.

SURFACE DETAIL (must be clearly visible on Stage 2 and especially Stage 3; Stage 1 shows a simpler version of the same texture): EVERY feather drawn individually: layered rows of overlapping feathers on wings, chest and tail, each feather with a visible center line and a lighter edge; small feather scales on the legs; hooked beak with ridges.
Creature concept: a flying bird, wings spread wide, long forked tail feathers, hooked beak, clawed feet tucked in, floating in the air (no ground line).
- Stage 1 (baby): chibi proportions: a very big round head, short stubby limbs, small soft rounded body,
  big round cute eyes, a few simple markings mixing black and violet, one tiny accessory (a tiny gold thunder-seal ring on one leg).
- Stage 2 (teen): NOT an enlarged copy of Stage 1. The body proportions CHANGE: smaller head relative to the
  body, longer limbs and neck, a leaner stronger build, sharper angular shapes, NEW body parts appear: neck and legs grow longer, the beak becomes sharper and more hooked, a second row of long blade-like flight feathers appears on the wings, the tail splits into 3 long feathers.
  Also glowing gold rune markings, a gold crest feather and gold bracers on the legs, partial black ornamental armor, and a little of the region effect
  (thin lightning arcs, dark storm-cloud chunks with outlines, glowing purple crystal shards, tiny gold sparks, small floating rock spires).
- Stage 3 (adult): NOT an enlarged copy of Stage 2. A completely mature, heroic, menacing body with a NEW
  silhouette: a much larger wingspan with a third tier of long blade-like feathers, a spiked crest crown on the head, an armored chest plate, big armored talons, a tail of 5 long streamers, feathered leg guards. It has the MOST DETAIL and the MOST VARIETY of all stages: every area of the body gets a
  DIFFERENT kind of detail (head, neck, shoulders, back, chest, limbs and tail each look different), mixing
  textures (fur / feather / scale / plate / stone), engraved patterns, at least three different rune scripts
  and patterns, gem inlays of different sizes, chains, tassels, spikes, bands, banners, glowing cracks, scars,
  layered armor of different materials, and asymmetrical details; ornate accessories (a black-and-gold crown crest, gold necklace with a violet gem, anklet rings, wing edges tipped with lightning blades); many region effects
  (thin lightning arcs, dark storm-cloud chunks with outlines, glowing purple crystal shards, tiny gold sparks, small floating rock spires) around it. Keep the silhouette and face readable: effects stay around the body, never cover the face
  or eyes.
EYES: Stage 1 has big round cute eyes with a bright highlight. Stages 2 and 3 have FIERCE, AGGRESSIVE eyes:
narrow angled eyes under a heavy dark brow ridge slanting down toward the nose, sharp slit-like pupils, a bright
gold glowing iris, a menacing glare (Stage 3 the most ferocious, with a thin gold light line). Every eye is still
clearly readable, at least 3x2 pixels, with a bright highlight on a dark socket, same gold color on all stages.
All 3 stages share the same species, the same color scheme (black, violet, gold) and the same key markings, but
the body shape and proportions evolve. Stage 1 is about half the height of Stage 3. Original design, NOT
resembling any existing Pokémon.

LIGHTNING AND LINE RULE: all detail lines (engraved lightning veins, rune lines, armor edges, feather and scale edges) must be CLEAN, SHARP and CLEARLY VISIBLE: at least 2 pixels wide, continuous (no broken scribbles), high contrast, pale yellow-white or gold lines drawn on the black and dark-violet parts, light-violet edge lines on the dark parts. Lightning is drawn as a few clean 2-pixel jagged lines engraved along the feathers / scales / horns / armor, plus a few short arcs that always TOUCH the creature (tips of wings, horns, tail, limbs) or link two crystals. NO messy thin scribbles floating around the creature, NO big fat cartoon lightning-bolt icons, NO large yellow wedge or spike shapes, NO detached floating bolts. Gold covers at most 10% of the creature (small accents only); the body stays mostly black and violet. More detail is wanted: dense texture and many fine details are fine as long as the main silhouette and the face stay clear.

NO text, NO letters, NO numbers, NO writing anywhere (runes are abstract glyphs
only).

Background: one flat solid vivid teal #00C2C2. The background color must NOT appear anywhere on the
creature, not even as a tint. No labels, no arrows, no shadow, no gradient, no
frame.
```
