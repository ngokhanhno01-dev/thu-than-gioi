# Prompt tạo ảnh vùng Nguyệt Cung (10 thú)

Bản chốt v4, soạn 17/11/2026. Khung theo `thu-moi/PROMPT_LAM_VUNG_MOI.md`, bắt chước `thu-moi/MAU_PROMPT_1_CON.md`. Dán từng khối vào Canva / Gemini, tạo xong gửi nhiều ảnh một lượt. Tên file ảnh: `nguyet-cung-NN-slug.png`.

## Phân tích vùng và chọn nền
- Ảnh vùng (đêm xanh navy, trăng lưỡi liềm, cổng chùa, đèn lồng sáng tím trắng, hoa trắng bạc). Bảng màu thú: đen xanh obsidian `#0B0D24`, navy `#1B2A7A`, xanh hoàng gia `#3F52C9`, tím oải hương `#9A93F0`, trắng bạc `#EAF0FF`, xanh băng nhạt `#BFD4FF`. Màu nhấn là trắng bạc (tối đa 10%), KHÔNG dùng vàng.
- Hiệu ứng vùng: tia trăng khuyết mảnh, đèn lồng đặc có viền, cánh hoa trắng, sao bốn cánh, tinh thể oải hương. Rune: pha trăng, chòm sao nối chấm, vòng chu kỳ trăng.
- **Nền: cam `#FF8A1E`** cho cả 10 con. Lý do: thú toàn xanh dương / tím / bạc nên cam là màu ngược tông; cấm cam, vàng, màu ấm trên thú để nền không lẫn vào thân.
- Con 10 là sói boss (người dùng chốt: sói chỉ là boss). Tên boss giữ tên cũ.

Pokémon-style pixel art evolution line sprite sheet of a DIVINE SPIRIT BEAST

facing left, all on the same ground line (rocky ground), clear

SURFACE DETAIL (must be clearly visible on Stage 2 and especially Stage 3; Stage 1 shows a simpler version of the same texture): EVERY fur tuft drawn individually in layered rows of pointed tufts on the chest, cheeks, legs and tail; ridged inner ears with vein lines; stitched pads on the paws; ear-edge notches.
Creature concept: an upright moon rabbit standing on strong hind legs, very long ears, a short crescent-shaped fluffy tail, holding a long stone pestle in the front paws; a stone mortar-like shield on its back appears from Stage 2.
- Stage 1 (baby): chibi proportions: a very big round head, short stubby limbs,
  small soft rounded body, big round cute eyes, a few simple markings mixing
  black and blue, one tiny accessory (a tiny silver crescent clip on one ear).
- Stage 2 (teen): NOT an enlarged copy of Stage 1. The body proportions CHANGE:
  smaller head relative to the body, longer limbs and neck, a leaner stronger
  build, sharper angular shapes, NEW body parts appear: the ears grow very long with crescent notches, the hind legs become long and powerful, the stance becomes upright, it holds a short stone pestle, the tail becomes a crescent-shaped tuft, small fangs show.

  armor, and a little of the region effect (thin silver crescent-moon arcs, paper lanterns with solid outlines glowing lavender, falling white blossom petals as small solid outlined shapes, small four-point stars, lavender crystal shards, tiny moon-phase glyphs).

  menacing body with a NEW silhouette: a tall upright warrior-rabbit with ears as long as the body ending in crescent blade tips with hanging tassels, a big round stone mortar shield on the back carved with moon runes, a long silver pestle staff, a thick ruff of silver-edged fur around the neck, layered black shoulder plates. It has
  the MOST DETAIL and the MOST VARIETY of all stages: every area of the body gets
  a DIFFERENT kind of detail (head, neck, shoulders, back, chest, limbs and tail
  each look different), mixing textures (fur / feather / scale / plate / stone),

  inlays of different sizes, chains, tassels, spikes, bands, banners, glowing
  cracks, scars, layered armor of different materials, and asymmetrical details;
  ornate accessories (silver ear rings, lavender gem necklace, anklet bands, hanging tassels, a black cloth sash with moon embroidery, small hanging lanterns on the staff); many region effects
  (thin silver crescent-moon arcs, paper lanterns with solid outlines glowing lavender, falling white blossom petals as small solid outlined shapes, small four-point stars, lavender crystal shards, tiny moon-phase glyphs) around it. Keep the silhouette and face readable: effects stay around the
  body, never cover the face or eyes.
EYES: Stage 1 has big round cute eyes with a bright highlight. Stages 2 and 3
have FIERCE, AGGRESSIVE eyes: narrow angled eyes under a heavy dark brow ridge
slanting down toward the nose, sharp slit-like pupils, a bright glowing pale ice-blue iris, a menacing glare
(Stage 3 the most ferocious, with a thin silver-white light line). Every eye
is still clearly readable, at least 3x2 pixels, with a bright highlight on a
dark socket, same pale ice-blue color on all stages. All 3 stages share the same species, the
same color scheme (black, blue, silver-white) and the same key markings, but the body shape and proportions
evolve. Stage 1 is about half the height of Stage 3. Original design, NOT
resembling any existing Pokémon.

MOONLIGHT AND LINE RULE: all detail lines (engraved moon veins, rune lines, armor edges,
fur and armor edges) must be CLEAN, SHARP and CLEARLY VISIBLE: at least 2 pixels
wide, continuous (no broken scribbles), high contrast, silver-white or pale ice-blue lines drawn on the
black and dark-navy parts, lavender edge lines on the dark parts. Moonlight is drawn as a few clean 2-pixel curved crescent lines
engraved along the body, plus a few short arcs that always TOUCH the creature or link two crystals. NO messy thin scribbles floating around, NO big fat cartoon
moon icons, NO large wedge or spike shapes, NO detached floating arcs. Silver-white covers at most 10% of the creature (small accents only); the body stays
mostly black and blue. Dense texture and many fine details are wanted
as long as the main silhouette and the face stay clear.

NO text, NO letters, NO numbers, NO writing anywhere (runes are abstract glyphs
only).

Background: one flat solid vivid orange #FF8A1E. The background color must NOT appear anywhere on the
creature, not even as a tint. No labels, no arrows, no shadow, no gradient, no
