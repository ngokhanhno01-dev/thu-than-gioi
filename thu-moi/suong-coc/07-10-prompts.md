## 07 · Lục Giáp (bọ cánh cứng, khe đá)

```
Pokémon-style pixel art evolution line sprite sheet. ONE original creature
inspired by a rhinoceros / jewel beetle (a stylized fantasy version, NOT a
realistic insect, NOT a spider, NOT an ant) in 3 evolution stages side by
side in a single row, left to right = Stage 1, Stage 2, Stage 3. Wide
landscape image, three equal sections, all in pure side view facing left, all
standing on the same ground line, clear empty space between stages, nothing
overlapping.

Art style: Gen 5 Pokémon battle sprite quality, crisp hard pixel edges, NO
anti-aliasing, NO blur, NO glow, NO semi-transparent effects, every pixel fully
opaque. The SAME thick dark navy outline on ALL three stages, same line weight,
clearly visible on Stage 3 too. Rich cel shading with 5-6 shades per color
area. Palette strictly: deep navy, deep teal, jade green, moss green, mist
white. The body must stay bright: mainly jade green and mist white, dark navy
only for outline and small shadows. No purple, no pink, NO neutral grey: horn,
claws and mandibles are mist white or moss green.

Creature concept: a low, wide armored beetle with a smooth domed jade green
shell (wing case) with a clean center seam, SIX short stubby legs (three
visible on the near side), a small head with one short curved horn and two
small mandibles, short antennae. A few moss patches on the shell. Keep the
design clean, few details, solid shapes, no floating particles, mist only as a
few small solid mist white wisps on the back.
- Stage 1: small round chubby baby beetle, same shell, same six legs, tiny
  horn nub, big round head, cute.
- Stage 2: larger, flatter shell with a jade pattern, a clearer curved horn,
  moss patches, stronger legs.
- Stage 3: biggest and most armored, thick layered shell plates with a moss
  strip along the seam, a large curved horn with a small branch, small solid
  mist white wisps. Only a few extra details, keep the silhouette clean.
EYES: on every stage the eye must be large and clearly readable: bright cyan
with a white highlight on a dark navy socket, at least 3x3 pixels, visible
even at small size. The same eye design on all 3 stages. No fake eye spots on
the shell.
All 3 stages share the same face, the same eyes and the same colors, each stage
clearly bigger and more elaborate than the previous one.
Size: Stage 1 is about half the length of Stage 3.

Background: one flat solid pure magenta (#FF00FF). No text, no labels,
no arrows, no shadow, no frame.
```
