# 05 · Vụ Hạc (Sương Cốc): prompt Canva

Khung: hạc. Môi trường: chim cao, lội nước. Dáng: cổ cong chữ S, chân dài thẳng, nhìn ngang, đuôi dải sương.
Rút kinh nghiệm từ 04 Vụ Điệp: giảm chi tiết Bậc 3, sương đặc và ít, cả 3 bậc cùng góc nhìn ngang (nhìn trái), không dùng đốm mắt giả.

```
Pokémon-style pixel art evolution line sprite sheet. ONE original creature
inspired by a crane (a stylized fantasy version, NOT a realistic crane, NOT a
swan, NOT a flamingo) in 3 evolution stages side by side in a single row, left
to right = Stage 1, Stage 2, Stage 3. Wide landscape image, three equal
sections, all in pure side view facing left, all standing on the same ground
line, clear empty space between stages, nothing overlapping.

Art style: Gen 5 Pokémon battle sprite quality, crisp hard pixel edges, NO
anti-aliasing, NO blur, NO glow, NO semi-transparent effects, every pixel fully
opaque. The SAME thick dark navy outline on ALL three stages, same line weight.
Rich cel shading with 5-6 shades per color area. Palette strictly: deep navy,
deep teal, jade green, moss green, mist white. The body must stay bright:
mainly jade green and mist white, use dark navy only for the outline and small
shadows. No purple, no pink, NO neutral grey.

Creature concept: a misty valley crane with a long S-curved neck, long thin
straight legs, a sharp slightly curved beak, and feathers shaped like moss
covered leaves. The tail is a few solid ribbon-like wisps of mist white (drawn
as solid opaque pixel shapes, not fog). Keep the design clean and readable,
few details, solid shapes, no floating particles.
- Stage 1: small round chick-like crane, short neck, short stubby legs, tiny
  leaf-shaped wing feathers, big round head, cute.
- Stage 2: taller, longer S-curved neck and legs, jade leaf feathers with a
  few moss patches, a small leaf crest on the head, two short tail wisps.
- Stage 3: tallest and most majestic, very long elegant neck, wide folded
  wings of layered leaf feathers with moss along the edges, a larger leaf
  crest, three long ribbon tail wisps. Only a few extra details, keep the
  silhouette clean.
EYES: on every stage the eye must be large and clearly readable: bright cyan
with a white highlight on a dark navy socket, at least 3x3 pixels, visible
even at small size. The same eye design on all 3 stages. No fake eye spots on
wings or body.
All 3 stages share the same face, the same eyes and the same colors, each
stage clearly bigger and more elaborate than the previous one.
Size: Stage 1 is about half the height of Stage 3.

Background: one flat solid pure magenta (#FF00FF). No text, no labels,
no arrows, no shadow, no frame.
```
