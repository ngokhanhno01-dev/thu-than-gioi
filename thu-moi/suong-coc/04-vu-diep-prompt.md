# 04 Vụ Điệp: thiết kế + prompt (Sương Cốc)

- Khung: **bướm đêm** (bản giả tưởng). Môi trường: trên trời. Dáng: côn trùng bay, hai cánh to đối xứng chiếm phần lớn dáng, thân nhỏ mập phủ lông trắng sương, râu lông vũ. Khác 01-03 (thú 4 chân và cá).
- Họa tiết cánh: gân lá ngọc + mép cánh là vảy sương, KHÔNG dùng đốm mắt giả.
- Tên không trùng quái cũ.

## Prompt (dán vào Canva)
```
Pokémon-style pixel art evolution line sprite sheet. ONE original creature
inspired by a moth (a stylized fantasy version, not a realistic copy) in 3
evolution stages side by side in a single row, left to right = Stage 1,
Stage 2, Stage 3. Wide landscape image, three equal sections, all facing
left, all floating in the air at the same height line, clear empty space
between stages, nothing overlapping.

Art style: Gen 5 Pokémon battle sprite quality, crisp hard pixel edges, NO
anti-aliasing, NO blur, NO glow, NO semi-transparent effects, every pixel fully
opaque. The SAME thick dark navy outline on ALL three stages, same line weight.
Rich cel shading with 5-6 shades per color area. Palette strictly: deep navy,
deep teal, jade green, moss green, mist white. The body must stay bright:
mainly jade green and mist white, use dark navy only for the outline and small
shadows. No purple, no pink, NO neutral grey.

Creature concept: a misty-valley moth with broad symmetrical wings, a small
fluffy mist-white body, and feathery antennae. Wing pattern is jade leaf-vein
lines with a soft scalloped edge made of mist scales (NO eye-spot patterns on
the wings, so the real eyes stay readable).
- Stage 1: small, round and cute, short stubby wings, big round eyes, tiny
  feathery antennae.
- Stage 2: larger, wings broader with jade vein patterns and moss-green
  accents, longer feathered antennae, a short solid mist-scale trail.
- Stage 3: largest and elegant, very wide layered wings with crescent-shaped
  tips, jade crystal and moss accents along the wing edges, long curved
  antennae; any effects are solid pixels only.
EYES: on every stage the eye must be large and clearly readable: bright cyan
with a white highlight on a dark navy socket, at least 3x3 pixels, visible
even at small size. The same eye design on all 3 stages.
All 3 stages share the same face, the same eyes and the same colors, each
stage clearly bigger and more elaborate than the previous one.
Size: Stage 1 is about half the height of Stage 3.

Background: one flat solid pure magenta (#FF00FF). No text, no labels,
no arrows, no shadow, no frame.
```
