# 06 · Vụ Long (Sương Cốc): prompt Canva

Khung: rồng thần thoại phương Đông. Môi trường: trời / nước. Dáng: thân dài uốn lượn chữ S, 4 chân ngắn, đầu có sừng nhánh, râu và bờm bằng dải sương. Khác 05 (chim cao đứng thẳng) và 03 (4 chân thấp, thân ngắn).
Rút kinh nghiệm từ 05 Vụ Hạc: viền navy dày và đồng đều cả 3 bậc, sương là mảng đặc màu trắng sương (không xám, không mờ), Bậc 1 phải cùng dáng thân dài với Bậc 2-3 (không để Bậc 1 thành con vật khác), tránh màu xám ở sừng / móng / răng, ít chi tiết ở Bậc 3.

```
Pokémon-style pixel art evolution line sprite sheet. ONE original creature
inspired by an Eastern mythical dragon (a stylized fantasy version, NOT a
snake, NOT a Western dragon, NO bat wings, NOT a realistic Chinese dragon) in 3
evolution stages side by side in a single row, left to right = Stage 1,
Stage 2, Stage 3. Wide landscape image, three equal sections, all in pure side
view facing left, all floating in the air with the body in a gentle S-curve,
clear empty space between stages, nothing overlapping.

Art style: Gen 5 Pokémon battle sprite quality, crisp hard pixel edges, NO
anti-aliasing, NO blur, NO glow, NO semi-transparent effects, every pixel fully
opaque. The SAME thick dark navy outline on ALL three stages, same line weight,
the outline must stay clearly visible on Stage 3 too. Rich cel shading with 5-6
shades per color area. Palette strictly: deep navy, deep teal, jade green, moss
green, mist white. The body must stay bright: mainly jade green and mist white,
use dark navy only for the outline and small shadows. No purple, no pink, NO
neutral grey: horns are moss green, claws and teeth are mist white.

Creature concept: a misty valley dragon with a long smooth S-curved body
covered in jade scales, a pale mist white belly, four short stubby legs with
small claws, a head with two branching moss green horns, and long whiskers
plus a short mane made of solid ribbon-like wisps of mist white (drawn as solid
opaque pixel shapes with a clear dark navy outline, not fog). Keep the design
clean and readable, few details, solid shapes, no floating particles.
- Stage 1: small baby dragon, a short chubby S-shaped body, big round head,
  two tiny horn nubs, two short whisker wisps, four tiny stubby legs, cute.
  Same body type as Stage 2 and 3, just smaller and shorter.
- Stage 2: longer body with a clearer S-curve, two small branching horns, two
  longer whisker wisps, a short mist white mane, a few moss patches on the
  back, a tail ending in a wisp.
- Stage 3: longest and most majestic, a large S-curved body, big branching moss
  green horns, long flowing whiskers, a fuller mane of three or four ribbon
  wisps, moss along the spine, a tail ending in a large wisp. Only a few extra
  details, keep the silhouette clean.
EYES: on every stage the eye must be large and clearly readable: bright cyan
with a white highlight on a dark navy socket, at least 3x3 pixels, visible
even at small size. The same eye design on all 3 stages. No fake eye spots on
the body.
All 3 stages share the same face, the same eyes and the same colors, each
stage clearly bigger and more elaborate than the previous one.
Size: Stage 1 is about half the length of Stage 3.

Background: one flat solid pure magenta (#FF00FF). No text, no labels,
no arrows, no shadow, no frame.
```
